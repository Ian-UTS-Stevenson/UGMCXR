**Journals:**
- Organised Sound (Cambridge University Press)
Strong fit for music technology, musical interaction and embodied/technological musical practice. Explicitly accepts review articles

- Journal of New Music Research
Strong fit for musical interaction, instruments and music technology, with an interdisciplinary scientific focus.

- Computer Music Journal (MIT Press)
Strong specialist fit for digital musical interaction, new performance interfaces, spatial sound and VR; explicitly publishes surveys.

- International Journal of Human-Computer Studies
Higher-impact option if the review is framed around HCI, embodied interaction and controller-free XR.

- Frontiers in Virtual Reality
Strong XR-specific option and explicitly accepts systematic reviews.

- ACM Computing Surveys
Most ambitious option. Best suited if the review develops a substantial taxonomy/framework for controller-free gestural interaction rather than focusing only on musical XR.
